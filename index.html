Alberta Electricity Market — Exploring Alberta's deregulated electricity market through four key variables: Price (hourly pool price), Demand (electricity consumption in MW), Lerner Index (a measure of market power — how far price exceeds marginal cost), and Reserve Margin (a measure of supply adequacy). Data compares December (winter) and July (summer). Use the dropdown to switch views; the hour slider on the scatter chart filters to a single hour of day.
